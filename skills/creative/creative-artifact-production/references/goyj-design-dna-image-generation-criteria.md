# GoYJ Design DNA image generation / pick criteria

Use when Chris asks Go Youn-jung to generate, curate, or judge Design DNA / Midjourney graphics for Brand Asset use.

## Core standard

The goal is not “pretty AI art.” A candidate should be useful as design-system DNA and mockup-ready brand material.

## Positive criteria

- **Systemizable visual language:** color, surface, metaphor, composition, and rhythm can repeat across multiple assets.
- **Mockup power:** the graphic has enough surface mass, crop flexibility, and tonal contrast to work on posters, cards, banners, signs, packaging, and UI surfaces.
- **Non-generic AI taste:** avoid familiar AI-startup clichés, glowing blobs, generic gradients, and decorative tech noise unless transformed into a more ownable language.
- **Future One UI relevance:** connects to warm ambient intelligence, context, emotional steadiness, human-centered interface surfaces, or calm system behavior.
- **Brand expansion potential:** can become a palette, texture system, icon/metaphor system, motion language, or component background rather than a single isolated picture.
- **Material/physical plausibility:** when used in mockups, it can look printed, embedded, woven, glassed, or surfaced naturally.
- **Distinctive but calm:** strong enough to be memorable, but not so loud that it overwhelms the product/brand context.

## Red flags

- Looks like a one-off illustration with no repeatable grammar.
- Too much white/empty area for full-surface mockups unless the brief calls for restraint.
- Fine-line-only motifs that disappear on physical mockups.
- Overly literal AI symbols, dashboards, neon grids, robot/brain clichés.
- Composition depends on one exact crop and breaks when applied to different surfaces.

## Generation direction for GoYJ

Ask for families/sets, not isolated images: 3–6 variations around one metaphor system. Include intended usage surfaces in the prompt: wall poster, hanging sign, fabric banner, card, UI background, packaging. Prefer prompts that specify material behavior, tonal palette, negative space density, and crop flexibility.
