## Candidate clusters observed

### Ambient companion / AI OS
- Handheld phone, human silhouette, warm oval halo, floating personal-state cards.
- Strong candidate language: `Handheld Ambient Companion OS`, `AI around the hand`, `context halo`, `floating personal-state cards`.
- Caution: emotion/social profiling needs privacy, consent, correction, mute/delete controls.

### Calm AI processing / state components
- Dot-matrix generation screens, progress dots, pause/stop controls, glass capsule progress bar.
- Candidate language: `Calm AI Processing UI`, `Calm AI State Component`, `pause-able AI state`, `progress without pressure`.
- Caution: avoid fake precision; determinate progress needs honest semantics and accessible labels.

### Luminous presence / boundary
- White or pastel orbs, chromatic rims, prismatic contact fields, safe halos.
- Candidate language: `Calm Luminous Boundary`, `Prismatic Contact Field`, `Light-theme Gentle AI Aura`, `safe AI presence`.
- Caution: avoid generic lens/bubble/prism aesthetics by defining rim thickness, color-ratio, blur, and center emptiness.

### Loop / orbit / feedback
- Helix lantern stacks, soft loop cores, orbital constellations, orange orbit trails.
- Candidate language: `Looped Warm Intelligence Core`, `Soft Looped Core`, `Orbital Agent Constellation`, `feedback orbit`.
- Caution: orange/black can read as alarm/heat; use warm feedback framing and restrained contrast.

### Relational mesh / emotional fields
- Overlapping mesh spheres/orbs, warm emotional mesh, protected shadow voids.
- Candidate language: `Soft Relational Intelligence`, `Warm Emotional Mesh`, `Protected Emotional Orbit`, `woven signal ecosystem`.
- Caution: red/orange/magenta intensity can imply warning/overload; define opacity, crop, shadow ratio, and privacy framing.

### Human-scale circular navigation
- Circular content cards, biomorphic frog imagery, thumb-safe controls, Explore pill.
- Candidate language: `Human-scale Circular Navigation`, `Biomorphic Circular Content System`, `thumb-safe interaction field`.
- Caution: requires usability/accessibility validation: contrast, target size, thumb reach, information density.

### Product narrative / design-system boards
- Rounded bento story cards, launch/poster surfaces, token/specimen card collages.
- Candidate language: `Story-based Product OS`, `Rounded Bento Narrative`, `Living Design-system Board`.
- Caution: avoid generic SaaS copy and unreadable blur; define Chris/Hermes/OBD-specific narrative language.

### Bold manifesto / material overlay
- Large cropped uppercase typography, chrome blobs, coral organic shapes.
- Candidate language: `Bold Manifesto Typography + Material Overlay`, `type as background architecture`, `soft chrome overlay`.
- Caution: do not reuse third-party literal wording or brand identity; extract only scale, occlusion, layer logic, and material relationships.

## Synthesis guidance

When asked to produce the final `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, do not dump every entry. Cluster repeated signals, choose likely core vs support motifs, and explicitly separate:

- product UI grammar
- brand/hero/motion assets
- package/surface motifs
- privacy/agency rules
- accessibility/usability validation items
- originality guardrails
