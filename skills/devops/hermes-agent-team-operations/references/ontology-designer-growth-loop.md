# Ontology Designer Growth Loop Pattern

Session pattern for Chris/Karina-led agent teams when Chris defines a future personal/professional growth goal and wants Son planning plus Muyeol validation before final reporting.

## Trigger

Use when Chris says a future workstream should help him grow into a role or capability, especially when it must start after a date and should be planned by Son and validated by Muyeol.

Example framing from the session:
- Goal after 2026-07-03: grow Chris into an ontology designer.
- Chris's meaning: concepts for understanding the world, concepts for understanding work, and concepts for communicating with stakeholders.
- Not the goal: directly making polished design artifacts.
- Desired 4-week artifact: a model diagram.
- Constraint: preserve existing Karina/Yuna/Go Youn-jung briefings; use only remaining token/attention budget.
- Education layer: Chris teaches junior designers, schools, and corporate employees, and wants the growth story to become future education storytelling.

## Operating Pattern

1. **Initial PM draft**
   - Ask Son for: goal interpretation, preparation timeline until the start date, 4-week loop, questions for Chris, and Karina-facing summary.

2. **Clarify without over-questioning**
   - Ask only the few questions that materially change the plan.
   - In this session, the decisive questions were: focus/domain, token/time budget, and desired artifact.

3. **Refine from Chris's framing**
   - If Chris reframes from product/design artifact toward conceptual growth, update the plan language immediately.
   - For ontology-designer growth, use: world understanding, work understanding, stakeholder communication.

4. **Add education-storytelling lightly**
   - Do not create a full course or lecture deck unless asked.
   - Add a weekly “education story card” with:
     - actual work scene or concept problem,
     - model insight,
     - connection to long-term One UI AI direction,
     - one teachable sentence for juniors/students/corporate employees,
     - Chris's personal growth-story note.

5. **Final review and scheduled reporting**
   - Schedule a one-shot final plan job on the requested date.
   - The job should ask Son for the final plan and Muyeol for validation.
   - Muyeol review scope: scope creep, weak logic, privacy/security/reputation risk, overclaiming, realism under token constraints.
   - Karina final DM: concise Korean synthesis with final goal, 4-week plan, operating rhythm, final artifact, education layer, Muyeol review result, and what starts next.

## 4-Week Loop Template

- **Week 1: Concept collection and boundary-setting**
  - Output: concept list, one-sentence definitions, confusing concept pairs.

- **Week 2: Relationship modeling**
  - Output: first relationship diagram; relation types such as inclusion, cause/effect, tension, priority, stakeholder interpretation difference.

- **Week 3: Work and stakeholder communication connection**
  - Output: stakeholder explanation variants, decision criteria, communication problems the model reduces.

- **Week 4: Model diagram completion**
  - Output: one model diagram, concept definitions, relation explanation, stakeholder communication guide, next questions.

## Token-Saving Guardrails

- Weekly output cap: one model-diagram fragment + one education story card.
- Avoid long research by default; use bounded research only when it materially improves the model.
- Keep Chris's weekly input to a 5-minute prompt when possible.
- The 4-week target is a conceptual model and reusable growth narrative, not a finished educational product.

## Slack Summary Pattern

When Chris asks to leave a summary in Slack, post a concise team-visible note:
- Goal
- First 4-week direction
- Education layer
- Operating principles
- Final review ownership: Son plan + Muyeol validation + Karina synthesis

Do not expose raw logs, tokens, private IDs, or internal command output.
