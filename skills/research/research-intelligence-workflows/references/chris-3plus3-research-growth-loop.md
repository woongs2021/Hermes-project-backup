# Chris 3+3 Research Growth Loop

Session-derived operating model for Chris Park's recurring AI/UX/design paper briefings.

## Purpose

The loop is not a forced One UI application pipeline. Chris already knows his core work deeply. The purpose is to use discovered papers and current research/trend signals to grow Chris's concepts, language, questions, and judgment as an ontology designer.

## Ownership

- **Yuna**: daily AI / AI×design / LLM / agent / human-centered AI paper/research discovery. Exactly 3 concise items when this loop is active.
- **Go Youn-jung**: daily UX / HCI / branding / design-management / design-education discovery. Exactly 3 concise items when this loop is active.
- **Muyeol**: daily question risk review and Friday validation/risk. For daily questions, review Karina’s proposed growth angle for overreach, emotional intrusiveness, sensitive-topic risk, and forced One UI linkage. For Friday papers, produce a local memo for Karina, not the final user-facing report: source plausibility, access level, overclaim reduction, duplicate handling, Korean-source coverage, sensitive-topic cautions, GO/HOLD/WATCH.
- **Karina**: final synthesis. For daily questions, frame the growth angle from the day’s research and hand it to Muyeol/Son; for Friday, prepare the final report for Chris and Slack after Muyeol validation; do not replace Son's coaching role.
- **Son**: daily growth questions/dialogue. Send 1–2 lightweight Telegram DM questions labeled “오늘의 성장 질문” based on Karina’s framing and Muyeol’s guardrails. Keep answers easy for a busy workday; these replies become evidence for weekly/monthly growth assessment.

## Daily question flow

After the daily Yuna and Go Youn-jung research:

1. Karina prepares a local memo with the day’s 1–2 most useful growth angles and question candidates.
2. Muyeol reviews the candidates for overclaiming, sensitivity, emotional intrusiveness, workday burden, and forced One UI linkage.
3. Son sends Chris a Telegram DM labeled “오늘의 성장 질문” with 1–2 questions max and a note that a short answer is enough.
4. Weekly and monthly reports should search for these daily questions and Chris’s replies. Assess growth from actual answers when available; if answers are sparse, say so and avoid overclaiming.

## Relevance weighting

- Primary relevance filters: Chris growth beyond familiar One UI work, HCI/AI strategy, brand/design quality, design education, design leadership, business/design-management judgment, and OBD/ontology-designer growth.
- Warm human AI UX, emotional stability, and psychological safety are **secondary lenses**, not default framing. Use them only when the source itself has strong evidence or a clear contribution there.
- When writing “why it grows Chris,” avoid repeating “warm AI UX” as the answer for every item. Prefer sharper language about what concept, market/category judgment, stakeholder tension, design-quality criterion, or leadership question the item gives Chris.

## Weekly Friday flow

1. Karina prepares the 3+3 shortlist from the week's Yuna/Go Youn-jung candidate files.
2. Muyeol validates the shortlist locally and adds guardrails.
3. Karina writes the final weekly report to Telegram + Slack, including a modest growth assessment based on Chris’s actual replies to Son’s daily questions.
4. Karina appends the final selected 3+3 to `/opt/data/agent-team-work-log/paper-candidates/weekly-selected-papers.md` with source/link/ID, Chris growth relevance, Muyeol status/caution, Korean-source coverage, de-dup notes, and monthly synthesis hooks. Monthly reports should read this log first, then use candidate files and cron outputs as supporting evidence. Preserve direct paper/source URLs whenever available so a future Faker dashboard can open each original source from the paper card.

## Weekly report shape

Start with one front-loaded paragraph answering: **why this 3+3 set matters for Chris's growth this week**. It should say what Chris can see, ask, or think more clearly after the week — not how the items apply to One UI.

Then include:

- 3 Yuna-lane items.
- 3 Go Youn-jung-lane items.
- For each: title, source/link/ID, core insight, why it grows Chris, Muyeol status, key caution.
- “Chris 답변에서 보인 성장 신호” — based on actual replies to Son’s daily questions; if sparse, state that modestly.
- “이번 주 Chris에게 남길 3문장”.
- “Muyeol 반영 메모”.
- “Son에게 넘길 다음 성장 대화 씨앗”.
- Warm Karina close.

## Monthly / last-day report shape

On the **last day of each month** regardless of weekday, Karina should synthesize:

- How the month’s research and Son’s daily growth-question dialogue changed or expanded Chris's thinking.
- Evidence from Chris’s actual answers where available; if answers are sparse, avoid overclaiming.
- 3–5 core growth themes.
- How Chris grew as an ontology designer: mapping concepts, stakeholder tensions, AI/UX risks, and design language.
- 5–7 important paper/trend clusters, not every item.
- Muyeol cautions: overclaim, source access, privacy/safety, hype/duplication.
- Son's next-month dialogue themes.
- What to continue, stop, and adjust next month.

## Pitfalls

- Do not make One UI the organizing frame unless Chris explicitly asks.
- Do not let Karina take over Son's growth-dialogue ownership.
- Do not let Muyeol's validation become the user-facing voice; Karina synthesizes it.
- Do not overstate abstract/metadata-only papers.
- Do not show duplicate papers as separate visible bullets; use repeat frequency only as a weighting signal.
